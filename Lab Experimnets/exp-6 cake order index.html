Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order a cake?

Select a cake, customize its size and message, add it to the cart, and complete the checkout form.

Can I track my order?

Log in and check the My Orders section for the current demo delivery status.

Can I change the delivery address?

You can enter the correct address when placing a new order. Existing orders can be managed by the demo administrator.

Are payments processed online?

No. Payment is simulated for this lab demonstration.

Privacy Policy

This educational website stores demonstration account, cart, and order data in browser local storage. It does not send information to a server or process real payments. Do not enter real passwords or sensitive personal information.
