Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reserve a ticket?

Register, log in, search trains, select a train and class, enter passenger details, and confirm.

What is a PNR number?

PNR stands for Passenger Name Record. This demo generates a reference number for each reservation.

Can I cancel a reservation?

Yes. Log in and cancel your ticket from My Reservations.

Can I get a refund?

This demo calculates an estimated refund based on a simple cancellation rule.

Privacy Policy

This demo stores account and reservation data in your browser. It does not connect to a railway server or payment gateway. Do not enter real passwords or sensitive personal information.
