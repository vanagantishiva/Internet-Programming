Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a flight?

Search flights, select a flight and seat, enter passenger details, and complete the simulated payment.

Can I print my ticket?

Yes. Open your ticket and click Print Ticket.

Can I cancel a ticket?

Log in and cancel your reservation in the My Tickets section.

Are real payments processed?

No. Payments are simulated for this lab experiment.

Privacy Policy

This educational demonstration stores sample account, ticket, and feedback data in your browser's local storage. It does not connect to airlines or payment gateways. Do not enter real passwords or payment details.
