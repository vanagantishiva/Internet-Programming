Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a ticket?

Search buses, choose a bus and seat, enter passenger details, and confirm the booking.

Can I cancel my ticket?

Log in and use the cancellation option in My Bookings.

Is online payment available?

This lab demonstration simulates booking only. No actual payment is collected.

Can I see my booking history?

Log in with the same demo account to view your bookings.

Privacy Policy

This educational website stores demo account and booking information in your browser's local storage. It does not send data to a server. Do not enter real passwords or sensitive personal information. Browser data may be cleared at any time.
